## About Me

Hello! My name is Yongtai Yin. I am currently a second-year Ph.D. student in the [Department of Electronic Engineering](https://www.ee.cuhk.edu.hk/) at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/) (CUHK), advised by Prof. [Wing-Kin Ma](https://www.ee.cuhk.edu.hk/~wkma/). I am a member of the [DSP & Speech Technology Laboratory](http://dsp.ee.cuhk.edu.hk/).

Before joining CUHK, I received my B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees in Communication Engineering from [Northwestern Polytechnical University](https://en.nwpu.edu.cn/) (NWPU) in 2021 and 2024, respectively.

## Research Interests
